We chose to measure text at build time rather than at render time, and the reasons are worth writing down because the decision looks wrong at first glance. A renderer that wraps text on the fly is convenient, but convenience is precisely the property we cannot afford: two machines with slightly different font fallbacks, hinting or rounding produce two different line breaks, and a line break moves every pixel below it. Measuring against the engine's own glyph tables during the build, and baking the resulting breaks into the generated source, means the composition that ships already contains its newlines. The renderer has nothing left to decide. The cost is a measurement build per composition, which we cache by charset so it is paid once; the benefit is that a frame digest recorded on one machine is reproducible on any other, which is the whole premise of the test suite.
