# Brief

## Intent

Owner-approved outline (2026-09-24): hook with no logo intro, the signature
effect being every character of copied text flying from its place in the raw
text to its exact place on the finished card (positions from Peesuto's own
layout engine). Then pain vs one key, "It reads what you copied" showcase of
six templates, one card in many forms, a real ⌥V chooser flow in a chat,
trust line, outro.

## Constraints

- X autoplays muted: every piece of information is on screen.
- Refined motion (Apple keynote / Linear / Arc): smooth easing, depth, masked
  reveals, kinetic type, camera push-ins. No particles, neon, lens flares,
  gradient text.
- Small, quiet Mac utility; neutral English examples, Pocket Motion content
  where personal flavour helps.
- Real assets only: cards from the engine and its layout; UI rebuilt in HTML
  after the app's screenshots; music synthesised here.

## Notes

- Owner feedback after the first cut (2026-09-24): "really stunning", BGM "perfect", but too fast at key moments. Re-timed with reading holds: raw text ≥ 1.2 s (≈ 2 s for longer), landed cards ≥ 1.2 s with the name label, captions ≥ 1.5 s, forms states ≥ 1.2 s; flights unchanged; ≤ ~60 s, so the showcase terminal item merged into the hook.

- Autonomous run: no storyboard review; the owner reviews the rendered file.
