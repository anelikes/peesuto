Welcome to Pocket Paste

Everything you copy shows up in a panel behind one shortcut. Pick an item, press ⏎, and it is pasted where you were. Actions such as Paste as card turn the text into something else first.

Pocket Paste needs Accessibility access to paste for you (it presses ⌘V on your behalf) and, later, to see what you are typing into.

Accessibility

Checking…

System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → turn on Pocket Paste. Required for pasting; the shortcut works without it.

Shortcut

Click the field and press a combination with ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥.
