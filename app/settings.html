Welcome to Peesuto

Everything you copy shows up in a panel behind one shortcut. Press it in any text field: the item that fits where you are is preselected, ⏎ pastes it. Actions such as Paste as card or Paste summary turn the text into something else first.

Accessibility access is required. Peesuto presses ⌘V on your behalf and reads the focused field (its role, label and the text around the caret) to pick the right item. Without it the history still works, but pasting and smart paste do not.

Language changes apply immediately.

Accessibility

Checking…

System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → turn on Peesuto. Required for pasting and for smart paste; the panel opens without it.

Shortcut

Click the field and press a combination with ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥. Opens the history with the pick.
